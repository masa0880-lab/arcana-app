Rider-Waite Tarot Card Images
=============================

The 78 card images in this directory are derived from the Rider-Waite
Tarot deck (Pamela Colman Smith / Arthur Edward Waite, 1909), which is
in the public domain in the United States, the United Kingdom, and
Japan.

Source images: https://github.com/searge/tarot
License: The Unlicense (public domain dedication)

The images were resized to a maximum width of 600px and re-encoded as
WebP (quality 80) by scripts/convert-cards.mjs in this repository.

To regenerate:

  git clone --depth=1 https://github.com/searge/tarot.git /tmp/searge-tarot
  node scripts/convert-cards.mjs
